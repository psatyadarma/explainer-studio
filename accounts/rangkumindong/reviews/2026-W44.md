## Recommendation: replace the blocked daily Trial-Reel opening A/B
**Stop rendering and QA-ing B variants now.** They cost voice characters, a render and a QA round each day, and nothing has been compared since 23 Oct (0 pairs in 9 days). Replace with a **single-arm rotation on A, with hooks assigned by the writer's pick from a 3-style set** (this is the "alternating-day crossover" from W43, tightened using this week's data):
1. **Styles under test: question_reveal, shock_number, kamu_stakes** only (the only ones with posted data; myth, story and person_collage were never posted as A, so drop them from the rotation until the first three are decided). Keep the other three as text-only hook-bank rows (hooks.md `hook_b`), which costs nothing.
2. **Freeze voice = Pratama, slot = 19:00, subs = block** (block never underperformed; word has the P2-outlier problem) and alternate only standard/short by weekday. Cahaya and word subs pause until the hook test finishes; the 2.4x Cahaya gap is enough reason, and it can be tested in its own block later.
3. **Judge on YouTube Shorts avg view % (primary, since YT shows 900+ views on some days vs 40 to 400 on IG), then IG avg % watched, then shares+saves per 1k.** Compare only within a weekday-topic group, and require >=5 per style with no single video above 1.5x its style mean to call it "decided". At ~1 video a day that is ~15 videos, about 3 weeks.
4. **Add TikTok to Upload-Post** (first hook-level data: retention curve and 3-s hold) and re-test the Trial Reel flag when IG followers pass ~100 (27 now) and ~1,000; if allowed again, revive the true same-day A/B.
5. Rejected alternative: posting B as a second normal Reel 24 h later (duplicate-content and audience-overlap risk, halves reach per post).
Honest limit: with SD ~12 points between videos, even this design only detects big effects (>=25% relative). The cheap answer is to read the hook bank qualitatively, not to chase a significance call. Needs the user's OK before the next day's brief changes.
