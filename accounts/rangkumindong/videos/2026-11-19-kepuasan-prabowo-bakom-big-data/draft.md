# Draft: Kepuasan Prabowo "43 ke 62-63 persen", kata Bakom: angkanya dari mana? (19 Nov 2026)

**Topic + angle:** On Rabu 18 Nov, Kepala Bakom Syahganda Nainggolan said that Bakom's "pantauan big data" shows public satisfaction with President Prabowo rising from 43% to 62-63% after Listyo Sigit and others were arrested. Angle (d) "what is claimed vs what is not disclosed": we report the claim exactly, attributed ("kata Bakom"), then list what the statement does not include (data source, period, how "kepuasan" is computed, sample, margin of error) and the speaker's own caveat on causation. **We do not say the number is right or wrong**, compare it with other polls, or judge Bakom, the President or Listyo.
**Arm:** Pratama · 19:00 WITA · hook story · subs block · format short · no B (hook_b empty, rule since 9 Nov). Storyline: listyo-sigit-penyadapan (new side angle, not a chronology PART, so no PART badge; earlier videos linked in the pinned comment). Sensitivity **high**. Not a stock topic, no buy/sell disclaimer.
**Length:** 108 spoken words → ~43 s with Pratama (~150 wpm), plus tagline + tail ≈ 46 s. Slightly above the 45 s short target; if the take runs long, drop "dkk" clause words in l3 or cut l6 (its point is repeated in the caption).
**Story:** Pak Budi (illustration, "misalnya") reads one number on his phone (h1, hook-critic PICK A, 10/14). h2 opens the loop "Angkanya dari mana?"; paid off in l4 ("pantauan big data") and l5–l6 (what is not disclosed). Turn ("Tapi…") at l5 (~60%). l7 closes on Syahganda's own caveat (Liputan6 quote, trailing "ya" dropped). CTA asks what the viewer checks first: open question, no side.

## Verified basis
- detikNews 18 Nov 2026 21:50 WIB and Liputan6 18 Nov 2026 22:31 WIB, both from Bakom's written statement of Rabu 18/11: "Di dalam pantauan big data kami dari 43 persen menjadi 62-63 persen"; "Tapi ketika beliau ditangkap maka itu naik kepuasan kepada Bapak Presiden"; Kapolri Suyudi "hampir 90 persen"; "Saya nggak bilang itu terkait dengan Pak Listyo Sigit ya" (Liputan6); detik: he "tidak bisa menyimpulkan langsung" a cause.
- Neither article names a survey, data source, sample, period or margin of error.
- **Left out on purpose:** "Jadi 20 persen itu selama ini terdistorsi" (unclear what it refers to; quoting it without context could mislead); the Kapolri ~90% (caption only, to keep the short tight); other polls (SMRC, Indikator etc.) and the 14 Nov "One on One" remark (journalarta only, figures unclear): a comparison would look like a verdict. Bakom = Badan Komunikasi Pemerintah (both articles).
- **Wording care:** "belum dibuka / tidak disebut" (in the reported statement), never "tidak ada" or "tidak valid". Listyo dkk = tersangka; "ditangkap" is the outlets' wording; caption adds "proses hukum masih berjalan".
- **Re-check before render (~17:00 WITA):** whether Bakom has since published its method, or Syahganda clarified; if so, l5/l6 must change.
