Politik: semua angka diatribusikan ("kata Prabowo", "menurut Katadata"); tidak ada kata "korupsi"; Prabowo sendiri menyebutnya masalah manajemen (CNN). Storyline: new (dekat survival-mode). - **Rp480 T**: confirmed. CNN Indonesia (11 Nov 22:31) direct quote: "Kalau 40 persen dari Rp1.200 triliun itu adalah Rp480 triliun". Katadata/Investortrust carry the same 1/3, Rp1.200 T, 40% "hangus".
- **Rp400 T**: not used. Only in a harianenergi headline; not in CNN, Katadata or Investortrust.
- **"+Rp100 M"**: not used. Not found in any article opened.
- **30% vs 1/3 x 40%**: CNN also quotes him "30 persen APBN kita bocor", which does not match 1/3 x 40% (~13% of APBN). Not used. The only 30% in the video is the dated 14 Aug Nota Keuangan figure (30% of Rp1.200 T = Rp360 T, Setkab + Databoks), shown as an earlier, separate estimate.
