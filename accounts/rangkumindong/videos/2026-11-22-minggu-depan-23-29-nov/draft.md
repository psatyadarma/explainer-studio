# Draft: Minggu Depan 23-29 Nov: 5 agenda, satu penentu upah minimum kotamu

**Arm:** Pratama · 19:00 WITA · hook question_reveal · no B (hook_b empty since 9 Nov) · subs word · format minggu_depan
**Topic + angle:** the week's calendar, walked through day by day (Sen-Kam), with the held-back answer **25** (Rabu 25 Nov = batas UMK 2027) revealed after the turn. Opening = hook-critic PICK A (candidate 2): stakes-first question, Listyo Sigit named in line 2; his hearing date stays attributed in l1 ("menurut Bangkapos", single source).
**Changed from the idea:** "5 tanggal" → **"5 agenda, 4 hari"** (only 23/24/25/26 fall in the week; 25 carries two agendas). "Bikin gajimu berubah" → **"penentu upah minimum kotamu"**: UMK is a floor, and it is optional (gubernur's policy), so "gajimu berubah" would overclaim. US Thanksgiving (26 Nov) dropped to keep the count at 5 and the length down.
**Storyline:** minggu-depan (series, after 15 Nov), touching listyo-sigit-penyadapan, gibran-ijazah-mk, ump-2027. Sensitivity: medium-high (Listyo + wapres): attributed, neutral, presumption of innocence stated (l4), no VP names floated.
**Length:** ~180 subtitle words / ~215 spoken tokens incl. spelled acronyms → **~80-88 s** at Pratama's pace. At the 215 cap; if the take runs long, cut `d3` first.
