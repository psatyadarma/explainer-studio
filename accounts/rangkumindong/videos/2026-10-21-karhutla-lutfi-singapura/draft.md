## Topic + angle
Who is responsible for the haze? The video lays out each side's claims, with every claim attributed and no verdict:
- **Singapore** (NEA / Menteri Grace Fu): part of its haze came from fires on land held by 7 Indonesian companies. Warning notices went out 16 Okt, then 3 more on 19 Okt. Fines are being considered. Menteri K. Shanmugam (same day, 19 Okt): scrutiny will reach parent groups and their owners.
- **Lutfi** (Instagram, 20 Okt): 4 of the first 7 supply conglomerate groups whose parents are in Singapore. Singapore shouldn't write warning letters just to please voters.
- **Companies:** two *other* SGX-listed palm firms (not NEA-notice recipients) dispute the fires' origin: First Resources says some fires came from outside its land; Kencana Agri says the fire spread from surrounding land. No response found from the 10 NEA-notice firms. Bumitama gave no statement. Greenpeace-report and SGX-listed firms are kept **separate** from the 10 NEA firms on screen.
- **Indonesia:** Kapolri: 98 corporate cases in penyidikan, with alleged foreign involvement. Mendagri Tito: financial pressure is fine if it goes through each country's legal system.
- Context: BNPB 7.118 hotspots (dated 16 Okt), and 4 people died fighting a fire in Bromo (respectful beat).
