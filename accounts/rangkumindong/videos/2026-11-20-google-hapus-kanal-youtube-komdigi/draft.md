# Draft: Google hapus lebih dari 23 ribu kanal YouTube terkait Indonesia, Komdigi tanya dasarnya (20 Nov 2026)

**Topic + angle:** Google's quarterly Influence Operations Bulletins list YouTube channels terminated as "coordinated influence operation campaigns". Indonesia-related channels: 3.598 (Q1) and 19.389 (Q3), both described as Indonesian-language, supportive of the Indonesian government (August also "critical of critics of the government"); Q2 had a few hundred more with mixed content, pro and critical (Katadata counts 840, total 23.827; Google's Q2 entries total 1.115). Spoken: "lebih dari 23 ribu" (true under every count). Komdigi (official statements 19-20 Nov; 20 Nov by Mediodecci Lustarini, Sekretaris Ditjen Pengawasan Ruang Digital) says it supports fighting information manipulation but asks for the basis: account types, violations, and why it is linked to Indonesia; free expression must apply to supporters and critics alike; Komdigi met Google Indonesia and YouTube SEA on 19 Nov and asked for a written explanation; according to Komdigi, Google acknowledged the bulletin lacked explanation, cited YouTube's spam policy, and is coordinating with its global team. No public statement from Google itself yet. Angle (e) who's who + the jump to 19.389 in Jul-Sep: **what Google says vs what Komdigi says, no judgement** on who is right, on whether the channels were legitimate, or on who ran them.
**Arm:** Pratama · 19:00 WITA · hook story · subs word · format standard · no B (hook_b empty, rule since 9 Nov). Storyline: new (none). Sensitivity **high**. Not a stock topic, no buy/sell disclaimer.
**Fact-check:** FIX 1 (Q2/total) and FIX 2 (Komdigi-Google meeting) applied 20 Nov.
**Length:** 214 spoken words (≤215 for Pratama, at the limit) → ~86 s at 150 wpm, + tagline and tail ≈ 89 s. If the take runs over 90 s, cut l9 (it stays in the caption).
**Story:** Pak Budi (illustration, "misalnya") opens YouTube and a channel he follows is gone (h1, hook-critic PICK A, 12/14). h2 opens the loop "Kenapa?", paid off in l1-l2 (operasi pengaruh) and l5-l6 (content per Google). Soft turn at l8 ("Tapi menurut Komdigi…", ~57 %), main turn at l10 ("Tapi ini yang sering kelewat…", ~71 %; cutting l9 would bring it to ~68 %). CTA: open question, no sides.

## Verified basis
- **Google Q3 bulletin (30 Okt 2026, read directly):** Jul 6.888 kanal "linked to Indonesia" + 17 kanal kampanye multibahasa pro-pemerintah Indonesia; Agu 10.134 (also "critical of critics of the Indonesian government"); Sep 2.350. Content: "in Bahasa that was supportive of the Indonesian government and its administration".
- **Google Q1 bulletin (8 Mei 2026, read directly):** Jan 352 + 275 + 71 + 16, Mar 2.854 + 30 = 3.598, all "supportive of the Indonesian government".
- **Q2 (fact-check FIX 1):** Google's Q2 bulletin (31 Jul 2026) labels no entry "linked to Indonesia"; Indonesia-related entries total 1.115 and the content is mixed (some pro-government, some critical, some for a party or official). Katadata's 840 is its own partial selection. So the script says "ratusan lagi, isinya campur", the title says "lebih dari 23 ribu" (Q1 + Q3 alone = 22.987), and 840/23.827 appear only in the caption, attributed to Katadata.
- **Komdigi:** detikINET 19 Nov 19:30 WIB; Tribunnews and VIVA 20 Nov ~09:30 WIB (free-expression line in both). **Update (fact-check FIX 2):** ANTARA 20 Nov 11:46 WIB, CNN Indonesia 20 Nov 11:42 WIB, Katadata: Komdigi met Google Indonesia & YouTube SEA on 19 Nov; Mediodecci Lustarini (written statement 20 Nov) says Google acknowledged the Q3 bulletin lacked supporting explanation and could be misread, cited YouTube's spam policy, and must coordinate with its global team; Komdigi asked for a written explanation. No direct public Google statement found. Left out: Komdigi's challenge to use the same detection on judol/pornography (optional context, not needed for balance).
- **Number care:** Q3 is 19.372 (Bloomberg Technoz, without the 17-channel July cluster) or 19.389 (Katadata, Tribun, Harian Jogja, with it). We use 19.389 (Google's own sum, also CNBC, Kompas, Tribun). The 17 and some Q1 clusters are not explicitly labelled "linked to Indonesia" by Google.
- **Left out on purpose:** Katadata's line that some networks supported/criticised specific officials or parties (not in the bulletins we read; would invite party talk); "buzzer" (CNN headline wording, a judgement); the Komdigi-Google PP Tunas history (other dispute, would frame a feud); any guess at who ran the channels.
- **Wording care:** "kata Google / menurut Komdigi" on every claim; "dihapus/ditutup" (Google's action), never "terbukti"; Pak Budi's channel is never described.
- **Re-check before render (~17:00 WITA):** whether Google has issued its own public statement or the written explanation; if so, l12/l12b change.
