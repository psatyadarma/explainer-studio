Notes for the critic:
- **#1 is my A pick:** "kamu" + the thing people actually want (cut through the noise), plain words, picture (stack of headlines sorted into "pasti" / "belum"), and a gap the body pays off (l8–l9: presumption of innocence + who hasn't commented). Neutral: states only the status.
- **#4 is my B pick:** mid-scene, ordinary, no context first; the news arrives through Pak Budi's phone; ≤12 words over two lines; labelled "ilustrasi" on screen.
- #2 assumes a WA rumour wave I haven't measured; plausible but unsourced. #3 is vaguer than #1.
- #5 "Kepala Staf Presiden" is a loose short form of Kepala Staf Kepresidenan; risky in a sensitivity-high piece.
- #6 is 9 words before the payoff and slow.
- #7 is strong on curiosity but leads with the most explosive allegation; needs "diduga" in the first breath (it has it), still the least neutral picture.
- #8 is a good turn line, used in the body (l8), weaker as an opener because it argues before it informs.
