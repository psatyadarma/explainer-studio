# Draft: Listyo Sigit tersangka penyadapan, apa kata polisi (dan apa yang belum) (4 Nov 2026)
Arm: voice Cahaya · 19:00 WITA · hook A **kamu_stakes** · hook B **story** (Pak Budi, "ilustrasi") · subs **word** · format **standard** · storyline **listyo-sigit-penyadapan** (new, no PART badge) · sensitivity **HIGH**.

**Angle:** "versi polisi" chronology + who's who, strictly neutral. Every allegation is attributed to Bareskrim; full names attributed to detik only; initials as Kabareskrim quoted them ("LSP, ASN, dan EH"). The turn states the presumption of innocence; then what's missing (no response from Listyo, Istana silent) and the one government voice so far (Menko Yusril). No opinion, no "makar" framing (that is commentary by politicians/analysts, deliberately left out).

**Morning re-check (4 Nov, ~detik front page + IDN Times):** still **no response from Listyo or a lawyer**, and IDN Times: "Hingga saat ini, belum ada tanggapan dari Istana". **New since the ideas:** Menko Kumham Imipas **Yusril Ihza Mahendra** spoke for the government (3 Nov evening, detik): "Pemerintah berharap seluruh rakyat menaruh kepercayaan kepada Polri" and "Polri menyidik Polri, ya memang sudah jadi tugasnya". Added as l10–l10b. Not the Istana itself, so the script keeps "Istana belum berkomentar".

**Deliberately left out (unverified or excluded by the light fact-check):** 30 Okt report date, saksi/ahli counts, seizure counts (detik video: "11 HP", not used), pasal + "ancaman 10 tahun" (detik video only), "ulah oknum, bukan institusi" (detik video only), "makar" opinions (Hasto, Guntur Romli, analysts; Yusril: "bukan makar").

**Length:** 183 spoken words in A (181 in B) + tagline, about 85–87 s at Cahaya's ~130 wpm. Within ≤190 and 60–90 s.
