## Open checks for the full fact-check
1. Brent close on 23 Okt: US$115,18 (detik) vs US$117,38 (industry.co.id). The script says only "di atas/tembus 115".
2. **Rp376,8 T scope (checked 26 Okt):** it is the total subsidi + kompensasi, not energy-only. Kompensasi Rp154,4 T is energy (Pertamina/PLN). Subsidi Rp222,5 T may include fertiliser and KUR; there's no energy/non-energy split. So hook A says "Ratusan triliun" and l6 builds the total as "kompensasi Rp154,4 T + subsidi" without calling it all energy.
3. **Rp338 T dropped** from the caption (and the Rp526 T projection with it). Fortune IDN's Rp338 T allocation can't be reconciled with KabarBursa's "84,3% of the allocation", which implies ~Rp447 T for the total.
4. Riyadh is not mentioned anywhere; no fuel price rise is claimed.
