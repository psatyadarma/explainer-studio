# Draft: Dompet kripto "aman" dibobol dari toko (3 Nov 2026)
Arm: voice Cahaya · 19:00 WITA · hook A **myth** · hook B **kamu_stakes** (fallback from person_collage, logged) · subs **block** · format **short** · storyline crypto-hacks (continues loosely after Binance 2 Nov, no PART badge: different story).

**Angle:** myth vs fact + how it happened. Belief: a hardware wallet is safe because it's offline. Fact: Ledger devices bought in Singapore shops carried a hidden implant that read the recovery phrase and sent it out over mobile data; Ledger's own check couldn't see it; sales via resellers halted in 7 Asian markets incl. Indonesia. Neutral: no advice beyond what police/Ledger said, no verdict on Ledger.

**Length:** 94 spoken words in A, 97 in B (incl. tagline + end), about 43–45 s at Cahaya's ~130 wpm, so it stays at or under 45 s. Openings are the hook-critic picks (hooks-review.md); "dibeli di toko" was cut from l1 because h2/hb2 already say it.
