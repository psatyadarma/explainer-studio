# Draft: Minggu Depan 16-22 Nov, "Rabu 18 November" (15 Nov 2026)

**Arm:** Pratama · 19:00 WITA · hook story · subs block · format minggu_depan · **no B version** (standing rule from 9 Nov; `hook_b` left out of script.json).
**Topic + angle:** week ahead, built around one date. Per the W46 review, the person-faced "what happens next" beat leads: the deadline for the two VP names (around Wed 18 Nov, per MPR rule; plenary Wed 2 Des per Ketua MPR Muzani). Then the wallet beat on the same day (BI decision), then two US items that land before office hours (Nvidia results, Fed minutes). Angle (c) what it means for you, framed as one circled calendar date. Politics is strictly procedural: dates and rules only, no candidate names, no favourites, no reading of the President's timing.
**Storyline:** continues Minggu Depan (8 Nov) + gibran-ijazah-mk (after PART 2, 12 Nov) + rupiah. No PART badge (agenda format).
**Length:** 210 spoken words (tagline excluded, acronym letters counted) → **~85-88 s** at Pratama's pace (≤90 s target). Trimmed per notes: `m4b` merged into `m5`, `n3` second sentence dropped, `b1`/`t1`/`t2` shortened.
**Left out on purpose:** Silmy Karim hearing (date not confirmed); UMP (no date this week); RDG "17-18 Nov" dates (not source-confirmed, so the script only says "Rabu, BI umumkan"); APEC dates (unconfirmed, script says "lalu ke China").
