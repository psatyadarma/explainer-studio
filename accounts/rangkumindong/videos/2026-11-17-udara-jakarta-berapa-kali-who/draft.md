# Draft: Udara Jakarta pagi ini, berapa kali batas pedoman WHO? (17 Nov 2026)

**Topic + angle:** Jakarta's air quality on Tuesday morning, told through scale (b) plus what it means for you (c). The held-back answer (l4): PM2.5 at 05.00 WIB was 92 µg/m³; **if it stayed like that all day, that's about 6 times the WHO 24-hour guideline (15)**. Framed as a conditional, because an hourly figure is not a daily average.
**Arm:** Pratama · 19:00 WITA · hook story · subs block · format short · no B (hook_b empty, rule since 9 Nov). New topic under #karhutla. The video does **not** claim a cause (smoke, vehicles and so on), because none is sourced for today. Sensitivity low. Not a stock topic, so no buy/sell disclaimer.
**Length:** 95 spoken words → ~38 s with Pratama (~150 wpm), plus tagline and tail ≈ 41 s (≤45 s). l5 (yesterday's comparison) was cut; yesterday's figure stays only in the caption.
**Story:** Pak Budi (illustration, labelled "misalnya"), hook-critic PICK A, goes for a run at 05.00, the same hour as the reading. The loop opens in h2 and pays off in l4 (about 6 times the daily limit, if it lasts all day). The turn ("Tapi…") in l6, at ~75%: this is a one-hour figure, so what matters is how often mornings look like this.

## Fresh data (re-pulled 17 Nov; the 16 Nov figures in ideas.md were not used as "today")
- **IQAir Jakarta, Selasa 17 Nov 2026, 05.00 WIB (hourly reading):** US AQI 177, tidak sehat · main pollutant PM2.5 **92 µg/m³** · IQAir: "18,4 kali nilai panduan PM2,5 tahunan WHO".
- **WHO comparison (revised after review):** the video uses the **24-hour guideline 15 µg/m³** as a conditional: "Kalau seharian begini? Sekitar 6 kali lipat" (92 ÷ 15 ≈ 6.1). It drops IQAir's own "18,4× pedoman tahunan" (one hour vs an annual average doesn't compare fairly). The caption gives the annual guideline 5 µg/m³ only as context, with no multiple.
- **Yesterday (caption only; l5 was cut):** Senin 16 Nov 05.00, AQI 161, PM2.5 69.4 µg/m³ = 13.9× the annual guideline (IQAir via Koran Jakarta).
- **ISPU (KLH) 17 Nov: not obtained.** The ispu.menlhk.go.id fetch was blocked, and no 17 Nov ISPU article was found (the "17/11" Databoks result was from 2025). The video uses no ISPU figures, and Depok, Kalteng and Kalsel were dropped.
- IQAir's forecast for 17 Nov fell to around AQI 80 in the afternoon. By the time the video goes up (19:00 WITA = 18:00 WIB), the air may be much better. That's why every line says "jam lima pagi ini" and the caption gives the time. **Re-pull IQAir before render** in case the source gets updated or corrected.
