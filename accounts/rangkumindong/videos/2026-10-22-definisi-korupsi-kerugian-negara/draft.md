## ⚠ Changed since ideas.md: Luhut clarified on 21 Okt evening
After the 20 Okt remarks and the critics' replies on 21 Okt, **Luhut issued a written statement (21 Okt, about 19:00–21:00 WIB, Kumparan/iNews/CNBC): "Kerugian keuangan negara tetap menjadi unsur dalam UU Tipikor, dan uang negara yang diselewengkan tetap harus dikembalikan."** He says the UNCAC point was only a comparison. DEN's 4 recommendations are: (1) refine Pasal 2–3 UU Tipikor and Pasal 603–604 KUHP, mainly how they are proven; (2) a policy/business judgment rule for good-faith decisions; (3) adopt UNCAC offences that are not yet in national law; (4) an omnibus law on state finances. The hook therefore stays a question, but the video tells the story in order: claim → what the law says → critics → clarification → status. Without this, the original hook ("dihapus?") would be outdated.

## Topic + angle
What would change if "kerugian negara" left the definition of corruption, and what was actually proposed? Neutral and in chronological order, with every claim attributed:
- **Luhut (Ketua DEN), 20 Okt, Istana:** the President agreed with DEN's study (**Luhut's account only. No Prabowo or Palace quote was found, and none is used.**). The UN definition focuses on bribery and "tidak bicara mengenai kerugian negara". The current rule burdens Danantara.
- **The law, explained without opinion:** UU Tipikor Pasal 2 (enrichment by unlawful means) and Pasal 3 (abuse of position), both tied to loss to state finances/economy. UNCAC: a list of offences (bribery, embezzlement, trading in influence…); Art. 3(2) says the offences do not need to cause damage to state property.
- **Critics:** Ketua KPK Setyo Budiyanto (state loss must stay; the urgent gap is private-sector bribery, trading in influence etc.). ICW (83% of 2024 defendants convicted under Pasal 2–3). Academic Trisno Raharjo, UMY (agrees good-faith business losses shouldn't be crimes, but clarify, don't delete).
- **Clarification + status:** Luhut: state loss stays; the proposal is clearer proof plus a business judgment rule. Yusril: "layak dipertimbangkan", but not in Prolegnas. No draft RUU has been announced.
- **Current law (fact-check FIX):** UU Tipikor Pasal 2 and 3 moved to KUHP baru Pasal 603 and 604 from 2 Jan 2026 (UU 1/2023 Pasal 622). The state-loss element is kept.
Balance check: the change side gets Luhut (l1, l2, l9) plus Yusril's "layak dipertimbangkan" (l10). The critics get 3 lines (l6, l7, l8). The neutral law gets 2 lines (l4, l5).
