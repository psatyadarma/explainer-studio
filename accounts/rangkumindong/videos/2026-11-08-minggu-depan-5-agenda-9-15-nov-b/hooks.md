# Hooks: Minggu Depan, 5 agenda 9–12 Nov (+ BI 17–18 Nov), 8 Nov 2026

Arm: Pratama · 19:00 · A myth · B kamu_stakes · subs word · format minggu_depan. Storytelling: Pak Budi as a labelled illustration ("misalnya", ILUSTRASI tag); every date and number is real and sourced.

Read from hooks.csv first: best holds came from plain, human lines with a gap ("Uang negara hilang. Pasti dihitung korupsi?" 49.4 %, "1.700 penerbangan batal… Rute kamu termasuk?" 29.9 %). Weakest: jargon or a bare number first ("Rp18.046 per dolar…" 2.8 %), and the last myth hook ("Katanya dompet kripto offline paling aman…" 15.1 %, which ran long). So: myth line ≤8 words, no acronym in line 1, and the twist arrives in line 2. The last Minggu Depan ("Dapat bansos? Caranya berubah 10 November.") held 20.2 %, so I avoid repeating that bansos phrasing.

Fact basis for the hooks: the "transfer kena pajak 9 Nov" belief is circulating (the claim comes from Hashim, Kadin, 29 Oct); DJP (Inge Diana Rismawanti, 2 Nov) says it is not a new tax, the PPN rate does not change, and personal transfers are not what the system targets. DJP has not confirmed the 9 Nov date.
