## Topic and angle
What to watch this week, told through one ordinary week of Pak Budi (fictional, shown with an **ILUSTRASI** tag; every date and number is real). The myth opener takes on the forwarded claim that transfers get taxed from Monday. The DJP answer comes in the second line, then the week runs day by day. **Turn (~65 %):** the loudest item (Monday's tax) is the least certain, and the quiet one (desil data on Tuesday) decides who gets bansos. **Loop payoff:** the bonus at the end, BI's 17–18 Nov meeting and what it can mean for loan instalments.
Not a PART. It follows the Minggu Depan format of 25 Oct and 1 Nov. Tax 9 Nov and Perlinsos 10 Nov were already items on 1 Nov; this time it is the day itself, with DJP's 2 Nov clarification, which is new.

## Fact-check notes from the light check, and what I did
- **Pajak digital 9 Nov:** attributed to Hashim only ("Kata Hashim di acara Kadin"). DJP's 2 Nov statement (Inge Diana Rismawanti; Katadata + IKPI) is the twist: not a new tax, PPN rate unchanged, personal transfers not targeted. The script says the date is "belum pasti", and the caption says DJP has not confirmed it.
- **DJP +50% ke ±Rp16 T:** this is Bimo, 15 Oct, a separate event. It is **left out of the script and the captions** (captions must carry the same numbers as the video). If it's ever used, it needs its own attribution: "Dirjen Pajak Bimo Wijayanto, 15 Okt".
- **Perlinsos 10 Nov, 50 juta:** VERIFIED. Katadata (21 Oct): launch by Prabowo on 10 Nov, 50 juta **orang**, cash instead of goods, DTSEN desil 1–5. CNBC (1 Nov): Luhut says "kalau tidak ada perubahan". Kumparan (26 Oct) also gives 10 Nov. Note: our 1 Nov video used "48 juta keluarga" (that is the 2027 plan); this one says "50 juta penerima". Different figures, so don't mix them on screen.
- **MSCI 12 Nov:** VERIFIED. EmitenNews (5 Nov): announcement 11 Nov 23:00 CET = Thu 12 Nov early morning WIB, effective 30 Nov. RCTI+ (20 Oct) agrees. The script doesn't predict the outcome. Status decision by 30 Jun 2027 (from the 19 Oct video).
- **Sidang dr Tifa 12 Nov:** VERIFIED via detik (6 Nov): PN Jakarta Timur, Thu 12 Nov 09:00, aide AKBP Syarif Muhammad Fitriansyah as a witness. Witnesses for 12 Nov are Syarif and Aiman Witjaksono (detik). Dumatno is a witness at **Roy Suryo's** separate hearing on Wed 11 Nov (detik d-8740174), not at the Tifa hearing. The script keeps only "ajudan Jokowi", which is correct for both.
- **BI RDG 17–18 Nov:** VERIFIED (KabarBursa BI agenda). "6 persen" = economists' forecast (Investor.id 2 Nov, Faisal Rachman/Permata; Bisnis 21 Oct from events, not reopened). Said as "beberapa ekonom perkirakan BI rate" (full FC: a few economists, timing differs; BI held 5,75% on 20–21 Oct), and the instalment effect is conditional ("kalau iya … bisa").
- Dropped: IHSG 5.050,43 (not needed), the "Rp11 ribu" flash sale (Shopee's page doesn't state the price), the ANTARA "hoaks" label (not opened; I use DJP's own words instead).
