## Topic + angle
Weekly #5 chapter 1 ("Siapa wapres pengganti?") ends on "belum ada nama resmi". This cut keeps its frame (kursi kosong 12 Nov, sidang 9 Des, tenggat 25 Nov) and adds the new development since the weekly was written: Mahfud MD's argument (X, Thu night 19 Nov, reported Fri 20 Nov) that the 14-day rule counts **hari kerja**, so the deadline already passed on 19 Nov and the 9 Dec sitting "bisa tak sah" unless postponed. Angle (d)/(e): one word, two ways to count, and what each side has said. No verdict on who is right.

**Mahfud's full argument (read in detik, CNN Indonesia, Hukumonline, Beritasatu):**
1. The President's two names must reach the MPR 14 days before the sitting (Tatib MPR = Peraturan MPR 1/2024 Pasal 136 ayat 1; he also cites the UUD).
2. UU MD3 (UU 17/2014) and the Tatib define "hari" as hari kerja, not calendar days.
3. So if the names weren't in by 19 Nov 00.00, the MPR "mungkin, tidak bisa bersidang (dan tidak sah jika dilakukan) tanggal 9 Desember 2026".
4. Fix: postpone to 14 working days after the MPR receives the names (e.g. names on 20 Nov → 10 Dec), still inside the 60 days, "agar ada sinkronisasi antara ketentuan UUD, UU MD3, dan Tatib MPR".
Our check: 14 working days back from Wed 9 Dec lands exactly on Thu 19 Nov, so his date is internally consistent. Law text (fact-check FIX 3): UU 17/2014 (MD3) Pasal 1 angka 9 and Peraturan MPR 1/2024 Pasal 1 angka 22 both say "Hari adalah hari kerja"; Pasal 136 ayat 1 writes "14 (empat belas) hari". Article numbers are in the captions; on screen as small doc stickers. Posted on social media (X; also Instagram per ifakta) Fri 20 Nov (FIX 4).

**News since the weekly (20 Nov → 21 Nov 11:30 WIB):**
- Fri 20 Nov: MPR verification-team members Hinca Panjaitan (Demokrat) and Hidayat Nur Wahid (PKS) declined to comment (Beritasatu).
- **Sat 21 Nov (fact-check FIX 2):** Panitia Ad Hoc member Ahmad Irawan (Golkar): "Belum ada perubahan jadwal", Mahfud's view "kami pelajari di Panitia Ad Hoc", "yang prinsip sidang pemilihan tidak melewati tenggang waktu 60 hari". Sekretaris Abidin Fikri: Panitia Ad Hoc meets Mon 23 Nov. Now multi-source (Garuda TV, Memo Indonesia, ifakta, journalarta). No outlet reports an MPR rebuttal of the hari-kerja reading, so the script doesn't frame it as one.
- Bambang Wuryanto (Wakil Ketua MPR, head of the Panitia Ad Hoc): names "kemungkinan terbesar" Wed 25 Nov, time not set (CNN 20 Nov 22:47; Pontianak Post 21 Nov 11:27; ANTARA video 19 Nov).
- Golkar (Doli) and PKS (Almuzzammil Yusuf): Prabowo to gather coalition party chairs this weekend (21 or 22 Nov), no invitation yet (CNN 20 Nov). In the caption only.
- No names submitted yet; no postponement announced.
