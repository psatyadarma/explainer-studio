## Topic + angle (new: storytelling, user request 6 Nov)
A chronological story of one week told through real, sourced moments: **Selasa** three suspects → **Rabu** KSP job gone (Keppres) → KSPSI temporarily removes him as Ketua Dewan Penasihat ( until a final verdict, per its Sekjen) → **Kamis** the current Kapolri, receiving retired officers at Mabes Polri, calls it "murni dalam bentuk penegakan hukum" → **Kamis malam** a fourth suspect, initials SU (per CNN: Irjen Slamet Uliandi), named but not yet detained (per police: post-stroke) → **Jumat pagi** what is still unanswered (no response from Listyo/lawyer found; motive: Istana waits for the investigation). Characters: Listyo (centre, no voice yet), Kapolri Suyudi (institution's voice), KSPSI (his other world), Slamet (the new name). Stakes: how far the case reaches, and whether it is being handled as law enforcement.
**Story mechanics:** each beat ends on a small open question answered by the next (s4 "bertahan berapa lama?" → s5 "Sehari"; s6 "bukan di pemerintahan" → s7 KSPSI; s9 "apa kata Polri sendiri?" → s10–11; s12 "hari itu belum selesai" → s13; s15 "Kenapa?" → s16). Turn at ~65 %: s12–s15 (the day's second half, the 4th suspect who is not yet detained). Cliffhanger: s18–s20 → PART 4. Opening loop: A's "persaingan" quote is placed in its scene at s10–s11; B's "SU, belum ditahan" is paid off at s13–s16.
**No invented scenes:** every scene is a reported event with time and place (detik 15:48 WIB, Antara 20:01, CNN 20:37). No dialogue beyond verbatim quotes, no inner thoughts. **Pak Budi is not used** (fictional everyman conflicts with "real moments only"); hook B uses the real press-conference moment instead.
