## Top issue
**Idea #1's premise is wrong. The marketplace PPh 22 collection did not start on 1 Nov. DJP moved it forward to 1 Oct 2026** (DJP press release SP-23/2026, 1 Oct). The tax has already been collected for about 3 weeks. The hook "Mulai 1 November…" and the "12 days to go" angle are both false. The CNBC (1 Oct) and Metro TV (19 Sep) sources were written before the change.
