## What the audience shows
_Written by the analyst each Sunday from real IG/YT numbers (W47, 22 Nov). The writer weighs this next to the user's picks; it is a tiebreaker, not an override._
- Over-performed: a **named real person in the hook line, with a hard claim or a held-back next step**: Pengadaan "Kata Prabowo, Rp480 triliun uang negara hangus." (IG 1,287 views, 53.6%, the week's reach spike), Listyo P3 (1,545 IG, 54%), Gibran niat mundur / P2 (YT 3,841 / 3,404; IG 38.7 / 35.2%), a plain money question with the payoff held back (Korupsi P2, 49%). On YouTube a live political story reaches 1,000+ in 10 h even with an illustrative hook (Mahfud wapres 21 Nov: YT 1,310 in 10 h; tentara bayaran 1,285).
- Under-performed: jargon or statistic-first hooks (BI-Rate, MSCI, pasal: 3-15%); stakes-only "kamu" hooks (n=5, 17-27%); an illustrative "Pak Budi" hook on a politics/legal story with no face on screen (Kepuasan Prabowo 117 IG, Google/Komdigi 43 IG, the lowest dailies); repeat PARTs on a fading storyline (Korupsi P3 12%); the 7-minute weekly (#4: 166 IG at 72 h, 10%; #5: 21 IG at 21 h, 8.6%, but YT 138 at 21 h).
- Platform: YouTube carried 2.1x the IG views in W47 (4,319 vs 2,018; W46 was 6.8x). Politics and people stories reach YouTube at 400-3,800; markets, tech and gadget topics stay at 14-450. IG dailies sit at 110-240 views unless a named person and a hard claim carry them (Pengadaan).
- Format/hook/subs: 17-26% IG watched is the default band (23.2% without the Pengadaan outlier). Nothing is decided except voice (Pratama 28% vs Cahaya 17%, locked): standard vs short +9% (+1% without Pengadaan), word vs block subs +12% (+4% without it), hook styles story/myth/shock_number/kamu_stakes within 12% (21.1-23.6%). person_collage 47.7% (n=3, always a licensed face on a live political story, topic confounded). Storytelling ("Pak Budi") is average at n=7 (23.6%): it does not hurt and does not lift.
- Rubric: wallet does not track hold (fourth week; weight at the floor 0.5); surprise 2 tracked both hold and shares+saves in W47 (weight 1.25, low confidence). Total score does not predict performance.
- Followers: IG 72 (+1 in W47); reach spikes (16 Nov 1,114) have not converted to follows two weeks running.
- User picks vs audience: the user's politics/legal picks produced both the best and the worst videos; the best ones always had a named person in the hook plus a live next step, the worst were abstract or illustrative. Wallet-rubric items did not do better.
