# Storyline: Changing the legal definition of corruption ("kerugian negara")
Status: **ACTIVE** · Category: politics/law · Started: 22 Oct 2026 · Sensitivity: **HIGH**

## Handling rules
- Attribute everything ("kata Luhut", "kata Ketua KPK", "kata Yusril"). The President's approval is **Luhut's account only** until the President or Palace speaks directly.
- Current law: UU Tipikor Pasal 2(1) & 3 were revoked on 2 Jan 2026 (KUHP Pasal 622) and moved to KUHP Pasal 603 & 604. Both still require "merugikan keuangan negara". Never call Pasal 2/3 "aturan sekarang" without that note.
- Give both sides equal weight and equal visual treatment (same-size cut-outs/name tags). No party framing.
