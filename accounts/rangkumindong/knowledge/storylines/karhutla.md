## Handling rules
- Sensitivity high. Attribute everything ("kata Singapura / kata Lutfi / kata Kapolri"). Never call a company guilty; always say "belum ada yang diputus bersalah".
- Keep three company groups apart (never draw links between them): (a) Singapore NEA notice recipients (10 firms), (b) Greenpeace-report / SGX-listed palm firms (First Resources, Bumitama, Kencana Agri), (c) companies under Indonesian police investigation.
- Hotspot counts: BNPB (6 priority provinces) and Kemenhut SiPongi (national, high-confidence) use different methods. Never present one vs the other as a rise or drop.
- Deaths (Bromo): calm, respectful beat; no SFX, no accent colour; don't tie the Bromo fire to any named company.
